Cultivating computer skills in Benin City since day one.

PGCC Institute exists to make quality tech education accessible, practical, and genuinely trackable — for every student who walks through our doors.

OUR STORY

Named for growth — and built to prove it

Pachels Global Computer Centre was founded on a simple idea: a computer school should feel like a place things grow — skills, confidence, and careers. Our sprout-and-leaf mark isn't just a logo; it's how we think about every student's journey, from first login to final certificate.

Today, PGCC Institute — our training arm — runs eight career tracks out of our Benin City campus on the Benin–Lagos Expressway, blending physical classes with an online Student Portal so learning never has to stop at the classroom door.

Our Mission

To equip every student — regardless of starting point — with practical, verifiable computer skills that open real doors in the digital economy, through structured teaching and honest progress tracking.

Our Vision

To be Edo State's most trusted computer training centre — where students, instructors, and employers all rely on the same transparent record of what a PGCC certificate actually means.
